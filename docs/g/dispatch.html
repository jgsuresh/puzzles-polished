Dispatch

João Pedro Neto, 2002

What it is

A 13 × 13 territory game: you send a stone in a straight line from one of yours, and where it stops it grows a domino, tromino, tetromino or pentomino. Groups without liberties are captured.

Why it isn't playable yet

The starting position and exactly how the new shape is attached to the stone that stopped.

This game is on the two-player list of Mathematical Games, Abstract Games by João Pedro Neto and Jorge Nuno Silva. It will be added once its rules are pinned down.
