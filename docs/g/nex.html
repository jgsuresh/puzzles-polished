Nex

Red joins the top and bottom edges; Blue joins the left and right edges, with an unbroken chain of their own stones.

Nex is played on a Hex board with a pool of neutral stones that belong to neither player. On your turn you do one of these:

  • Drop two stones: place one stone of your colour and one neutral stone, on two empty hexagons.
  • Convert: turn two neutral stones into your colour, and turn one of your own stones into a neutral stone.

Either way you end up with exactly one more stone of your colour, so both players always have the same number after each full round. Neutral stones don't count for anyone's chain.

Red wants a chain joining the top and bottom edges; Blue wants a chain joining left and right. If the board is nearly full, a single stone is placed or converted instead, which ends the game.

How to play it here: to drop, tap an empty cell (your stone), then another empty cell (the neutral stone). To convert, tap two neutral stones, then one of your stones.

Passing the phone? Tap a square to preview your move, then press Confirm (or tap the same square again) and hand the device over.
