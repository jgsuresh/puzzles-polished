Aboyne

Paul Sijben, 1995

What it is

A 5 × 5 hexagonal-board game. Each turn you step a stone to an adjacent empty cell, or jump over a line of your own stones. A stone next to an enemy stone is blocked. You win by reaching your goal cell or by leaving your opponent with no move.

Why it isn't playable yet

The starting position and the goal-cell locations are only shown in a diagram.

This game is on the two-player list of Mathematical Games, Abstract Games by João Pedro Neto and Jorge Nuno Silva. It will be added once its rules are pinned down.
