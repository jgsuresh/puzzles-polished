Gogol

Gustav Levay, 2002

What it is

An 8 × 8 game with pawns that can jump to any empty square and a King that slides like a chess queen. Pieces capture by jumping over an adjacent enemy. Capture the enemy King, or reach the far row with yours.

Why it isn't playable yet

The starting arrangement: the sources we found disagree on where the Kings start.

This game is on the two-player list of Mathematical Games, Abstract Games by João Pedro Neto and Jorge Nuno Silva. It will be added once its rules are pinned down.
