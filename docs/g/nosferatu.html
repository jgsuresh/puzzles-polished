Nosferatu

Chris Huntoon, 2001

What it is

Twelve Villagers and five Vampires face one powerful Nosferatu on a hexagonal board. Pieces capture by jumping, and the Nosferatu can turn Villagers into Vampires. Capture all the Villagers, or capture the Nosferatu.

Why it isn't playable yet

The board size (the sources we found disagree) and who moves first.

This game is on the two-player list of Mathematical Games, Abstract Games by João Pedro Neto and Jorge Nuno Silva. It will be added once its rules are pinned down.
