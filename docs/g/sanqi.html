SanQi

L. Lynn Smith, 2003

What it is

A three-colour hex-board game: you drop a stone of any colour, or replace a stone that is outnumbered by at least two to one by a neighbouring colour. The players aim for different shapes of six.

Why it isn't playable yet

Precise definitions of the winning "circle", "triangle" and "line", and the board size.

This game is on the two-player list of Mathematical Games, Abstract Games by João Pedro Neto and Jorge Nuno Silva. It will be added once its rules are pinned down.
