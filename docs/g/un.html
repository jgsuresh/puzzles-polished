UN

João Pedro Neto, 2003

What it is

Players take turns sliding a single neutral stone any distance in a straight line over empty cells; every cell it passes is used up. The first player who cannot move loses.

Why it isn't playable yet

The board is only shown in an image.

This game is on the two-player list of Mathematical Games, Abstract Games by João Pedro Neto and Jorge Nuno Silva. It will be added once its rules are pinned down.
