Annuvin

Jeff Roy, 2001

What it is

A capture game on a 4 × 4 hexagonal board where each side has six stones. A stone can move as many spaces as 7 minus the number of stones you have left, so the last stone moves farthest. You win by capturing everything.

Why it isn't playable yet

The starting setup is only shown in a diagram.

This game is on the two-player list of Mathematical Games, Abstract Games by João Pedro Neto and Jorge Nuno Silva. It will be added once its rules are pinned down.
