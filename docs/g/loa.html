Lines of Action

Connect all of your pieces into a single group (touching sides or corners).

Black holds the top and bottom rows, White the left and right columns, and Black moves first.

On your turn, move one piece in a straight line (across, down or diagonally) by exactly as many squares as there are pieces, of either colour, anywhere on that whole line. You may jump over your own pieces but not over enemy pieces. You may land on an empty square or on an enemy piece, which is captured and removed; you may not land on your own piece.

You win when all your pieces form one connected group (squares touching by side or corner). If a move connects both sides at once, the player who moved wins.

Passing the phone? Tap a square to preview your move, then press Confirm (or tap the same square again) and hand the device over.
