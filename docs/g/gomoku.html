Gomoku

Place five or more stones of your colour in an unbroken line: across, down or diagonally.

Players take turns placing a stone on an empty intersection. Black goes first. Stones never move and are never captured.

The first player to make an unbroken line of five or more of their own stones, horizontally, vertically or diagonally, wins. If the board fills up first, it is a draw.

Tip: an "open three" (three in a row with space at both ends) forces a reply, and two threats at once usually wins.
