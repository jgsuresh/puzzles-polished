3 Stooges

Jorge Nuno Silva, 2003

What it is

A "towers" game where you swap the colour of lone stones or move the top stone of a two-stone stack onto a neighbouring lone stone. You win with three stacks in a row, or by stalemating your opponent.

Why it isn't playable yet

The board and the starting stacks are only shown in an image.

This game is on the two-player list of Mathematical Games, Abstract Games by João Pedro Neto and Jorge Nuno Silva. It will be added once its rules are pinned down.
