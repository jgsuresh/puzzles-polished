Hobbes

Dan Troyka, 2002

What it is

A 5 × 5 game inspired by Neutron. Each turn you walk your King one or more steps, then you must either capture the enemy King or push or pull an adjacent soldier. Capturing the King wins.

Why it isn't playable yet

The starting position is only shown in a diagram, and a few push/pull edge cases are not spelled out.

This game is on the two-player list of Mathematical Games, Abstract Games by João Pedro Neto and Jorge Nuno Silva. It will be added once its rules are pinned down.
